=== CHECK 7: THEORY (D)/(G) vs the Bradford divisor condition ===
  (added 2026-08-23; supports THEORY.md 8.2)
  Bradford: exists x with ceil(p/4)<=x<=ceil(p/2) and d | x*x with
    d = -p*x (mod 4x-p),  or  d <= x and d = -x (mod 4x-p).
  THEORY:   exists z' with (D) p | 4z'-1, or
            (G) exists sigma | z'^2 with 4z'-1 | sigma + p*z'.
  Both are the SFFT divisor condition of the same equation; they
  differ only in which denominator is eliminated first.
  45 primes tested, solvability disagreements: 0
  (agreement is expected and is NOT evidence of novelty: both
   conditions are equivalent to E-S for p, so this only confirms
   the two normalizations are the same construction.)
